Why this exists

A little offline fun for my son

Cipher Printie started at the kitchen table — not as a product idea, but as a dad looking for something we could do together without another screen.

I’m a father, and I love watching my son get absorbed in a puzzle. The hard part is finding activities that feel exciting and still leave the tablet in another room. One afternoon I wrote him a “secret mission,” scrambled the letters by hand, and watched him decode it line by line. He lit up. I wanted more of that.

So I built Cipher Printie: a tiny tool that turns a message into a printable cryptography mission. Choose a cipher, invent a silly spy story, hit print, and you’ve got a worksheet he can solve with a pencil. No accounts. No cloud. Nothing leaves the browser.

If you’re a parent, teacher, or anyone who wants screen-free brain games, I hope this helps you make the same kind of moment — a quiet mission, a shared laugh, and a decoded note waiting at the end.
